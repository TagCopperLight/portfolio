Urban traffic editor and simulator for modifying a real road network imported from OpenStreetMap, then measuring how the changes affect travel time and pollution. I designed the interface (Figma) and wrote a large part of the Rust engine: intersection handling, where vehicles must respect right of way, yield signs and traffic lights without colliding. Built by a team of 5 following a full software engineering process: planning, user testing and documentation.
